# Sprint Retrospective – Urban Tree Explorer (Sprint 3)

## **Overview**
| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Meeting Facilitator** | Scrum Master |
| **Goal of the Meeting** | Improve sprint processes and teamwork. |
| **Attendees** | Development Team, Scrum Master, Product Owner (Optional) |

---

## **What Went Well**  
- ✅ Successful implementation of the Park and Places of Interest location map, user-generated map market capabilities and heatmap with walking route;
- ✅ The Development team (frontend and backend) collaborated effectively to implement the map and login systems; and
- ✅ The team collaboratively reviewed all relevant documentation to proactively solve problems, and raise queries with the client (in particular regarding operation of the heatmap, and user-generated content management).

---

## **What Could Have Been Done Better**  
- ⚠️ **Start time**: The team's start time was delayed due to a need to review documentation for the WP GO Maps heat map functionality, and the functionality to embed YouTube videos via iframes.
- ⚠️ **Communication**: Notwithstanding the team's more proactive approach to notification of availability (following the Sprint 2 Retrospective), as with Sprint 2, the team were balancing a number of other pressures during this sprint, meaning there were some periods of inactivity that could have been better communicated to other team members.
- ⚠️ **Reliance on existing documentation**: The team was somewhat limited by available documentation and functionality of client-selected tools (e.g. the heatmap functionality in WP Go Maps and the ability to embed iframes for YouTube videos without further plugins).
- ⚠️ **Communicate more proactively with the Client**: The team was somewhat limited by response times for the client, noting they are managing a range of different products at once.  

---

## **What We Will Do Differently**  
- ⏳ Commence executing tasks **earlier**, based on our new knowledge of key systems/their documentation.
- ⏳ The team to **update each other via WhatsApp one business day in advance** when unable to complete tasks within required timeframe, to ensure resources can be effectively reallocated if necessary to keep the project on track.
- ⏳ The team to **raise queries earlier with the client** to ensure issues can be resolved promptly.
- ⏳ The team to **review alternate technologies** to provide further options to the client so as to mitigate the limitations of the chosen tools.
