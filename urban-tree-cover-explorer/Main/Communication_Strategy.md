# 💬 Communication Strategy
---
This document sets out how we will communicate while building the Urban Tree Cover Explorer (**UCTE**).  We will:

1. **Assign** roles and responsibilities for each sprint based on the Roles Documentation Template;

2. **Communicate** in accordance with our Communication Plan - meaning we:
   - meet during each Formal Meeting; and
   - align regularly via informal communication methods; and

3. **Manage** inefficient communication via the Communication Escalation Strategy. 
---
## Communication Plan – Formal Meetings - UTCE

| Owner          | Meeting Title  | Audience (Stakeholder)         | Meeting Objective                            | Frequency        | Format (Virtual / In-Person) |
|---------------|---------------|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------------|---------------------------------|
| Scrum Master  | Virtual Spring Planning Meeting | Development Team | Plan upcoming sprint, set goals and assign tasks | Sprint Week 1 Monday (9pm) | Virtual |
| Scrum Master | Face-to- Face Stand-Up Meeting | Development Team | Discuss sprint progress & roadblocks | Each Friday (6pm) | In-Person |
| Scrum Master  | Virtual Stand-Up Meeting | Development Team | Discuss sprint progress & roadblocks | Sprint Week 2 Monday (9pm) | Virtual |
| Product Owner | Sprint Review | Development Team, Client | Demonstrate completed work to stakeholders | After end of Sprint 3 (Friday 30 June) | Virtual |
| Scrum Master | Sprint Retrospective & Future Spring Planning | Development Team | Reflect on sprint execution & improvements | Sprint Week 2 Friday (6:30pm) | Virtual|

## Communication Methods – Daily/Ad-Hoc Discussions - UTCE

| Method          | Purpose  |
|---------------|---------------|
| WhatsApp 📱 | Daily discussion of ad-hoc matters; meeting scheduling; reminders |
| Git 👾 | Plans; formalised documentation; product materials |
| GoogleDocs 📄 | Meeting notes; ad-hoc workspace; note taking |
| Zoom 🎥 | Virtual meetings |

## Communication Escalation Strategy - UTCE
| Meeting Title       | Location    | Escalation Trigger                                  | Contingency Plan                                        |
|---------------------|------------|----------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Stand-Up Meeting | Virtual & In-Person | Meeting time for last 3 meetings exceeded 30 minutes | Scrum master to act as timekeeper; enforce a strict 5-minute time limit per person. |
| Stand-Up Meeting   | Virtual & In-Person | Development team members have not prepared / are unable to provide updates | Scrum master to remind team before each stand up to be prepared to share updates/blockers. |
| Sprint Review | Virtual | Client identifies non-compliant product | Product Owner to pre-align with Client, providing summary of existing sprint and key outcomes to set expectations.  |
| Sprint Review | Virtual | Client feedback / engagement is not forthcoming | Development team to, under Product Owner's guidance, prepare to a set of queries to guide meeting toward actionable items. |
| Sprint Review | Virtual | Development team is unable to present sprint progress clearly | Product Owner to arrange pre-meeting with Development team to align on key matters for presentation.  |
| Sprint Retrospective & Future Spring Planning  | Virtual | Few team members contribute ideas / conversation is dominated by a few individuals | Scrum master to "think & share" via anonymous form to bolster scope of ideas for discussion, or implemented time-limits on sharing. |
| Sprint Retrospective & Future Spring Planning  | Virtual | Learnings/action items are unclear | Scrum Master and/or Product Owner to maintain list of action items (and assignees, where applicable) for distribution following meeting. |
