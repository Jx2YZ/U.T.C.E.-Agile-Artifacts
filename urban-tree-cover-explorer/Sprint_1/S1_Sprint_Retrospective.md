# Sprint Retrospective – Urban Tree Explorer (Sprint 1)  

## **Overview**
| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Meeting Facilitator** | Scrum Master |
| **Goal of the Meeting** | Improve sprint processes and teamwork |
| **Attendees** | Development Team, Scrum Master, Product Owner (Optional) |

---

## **What Went Well**  
- ✅ Successful implementation of the map system, based on a trial (then actual) dataset;
- ✅ The team adapted to changing client priorities, ensuring tasks remained clearly broken down to streamline work distribution;
- ✅ The map development team (frontend and backend) collaborated effectively to implement the map system; and
- ✅ The team collaboratively reviewed all relevant documentation to proactively solve problems, and raise queries with the client.

---

## **What Could Have Been Done Better**  
- ⚠️ **Test Cases**: The team could develop test cases earlier to guide the development process.
- ⚠️ **Gold Plating**: At times, the team were keen to develop a product in accordance with industry best practice, rather than in accordance with the client's narrow requirements.
- ⚠️ **Start time**: The team's start time was delayed due to a need to review complex documentation.
- ⚠️ **Communication**: The team would benefit from knowing when people are updating documents/Wordpress, and what changes were made to ensure we are across key changes.
- ⚠️ **Meetings**: The team could have met earlier to plan for the upcoming sprint.

---

## **What We Will Do Differently**  
- ⏳ Develop test cases within the **first seven days** of Sprint 2.
- ⏳ Confine our development to only those tools/systems **specifically endorsed by the client**, to streamline our development process.
- ⏳ Commence executing tasks **earlier**, based on our new knowledge of key systems/their documentation.
- ⏳ The team to **update each other via WhatsApp** when making key changes.
- ⏳ Implement a sprint planning meeting on the first day of a new sprint **at 9pm**.
