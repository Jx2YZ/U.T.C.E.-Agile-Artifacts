## 🏆 **Stand-up Meeting Format**

<br>Note: Due to the nature of the task dependencies, members might not have continuous workflow and might not have answers to these questions at some meetings. 

1️⃣ **What did I do yesterday?**  
2️⃣ **What will I do today?**  
3️⃣ **Do I have any blockers?**  
4️⃣ **What are my next steps? (Action Items)**
