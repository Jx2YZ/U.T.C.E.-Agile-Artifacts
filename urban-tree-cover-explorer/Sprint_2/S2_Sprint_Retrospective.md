# Sprint Retrospective – Urban Tree Explorer (Sprint 2)

## **Overview**
| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Meeting Facilitator** | Scrum Master |
| **Goal of the Meeting** | Improve sprint processes and teamwork. |
| **Attendees** | Development Team, Scrum Master, Product Owner (Optional) |

---

## **What Went Well**  
- ✅ Successful implementation of the map filter and location-based search pages;
- ✅ The site is access-protected, requiring users to login with their email and password before accessing it (with incorrect logins receiving an error, and unauthorised users being redirected to the login page);
- ✅ The Development team (frontend and backend) collaborated effectively to implement the map and login systems; and
- ✅ The team collaboratively reviewed all relevant documentation to proactively solve problems, and raise queries with the client.

---

## **What Could Have Been Done Better**  
- ⚠️ **Specification Interpretation**: The team had proceeded with some preliminary development work without clarifying certain assumptions with the client, leading to a need to revise that work down the track (albeit in a limited way, given the preliminary nature of that work).
- ⚠️ **Start time**: The team's start time was delayed due to a need to review documentation for Wordpress’ login functionality, and complex documentation regarding the use of filter criteria for the map.
- ⚠️ **Communication**: The team were balancing a number of other pressures during this sprint, meaning there were some periods of inactivity that could have been better communicated to other team members.
- ⚠️ **Reliance on existing documentation**: The team was somewhat limited by available documentation and functionality of client-selected tools (e.g. the login functionality and map plugin).
- ⚠️ **Earlier communication with the client**: The team raised questions with the client fairly late in the sprint, meaning issues lingered longer than needed.

---

## **What We Will Do Differently**  
- ⏳ Commence executing tasks **earlier**, based on our new knowledge of key systems/their documentation.
- ⏳ The team to **update each other via WhatsApp** when making key changes, or when unable to complete tasks within required timeframe, to ensure resources can be effectively reallocated.
- ⏳ The team to **raise queries earlier with the client** to ensure issues can be resolved promptly.
